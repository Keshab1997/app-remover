Privacy Policy — Goodbye Apps

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Goodbye Apps ("the app", "we") is an Android utility that helps you review and uninstall applications installed on your device. This policy explains what the app does with information and what it does not do.

Summary

  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data.
  • The list of apps on your device stays on your device.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs in this version.

Information the app accesses

To do its job, the app reads the following on your device:

  • Installed application list — app names, package names, icons, sizes and install/update dates. This is displayed to you and is never uploaded.
  • Device memory information — available and total RAM, shown in the app's bottom bar and, if you enable it, in an ongoing status bar notification. It is not stored or transmitted.
  • Per-app storage usage — with your permission (usage access, turned on by hand in system settings), the app, data and cache size of one package you tapped. Shown only on your device.
  • App preferences — your theme choice and the status bar toggle, stored locally using Android's app-private storage. They never leave your device.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, files, location, microphone, camera, messages, call logs or accounts.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — to show the complete list of installed apps, which is the app's core function.
  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES — to open Android's system uninstall dialog when you ask to remove an app.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (optional) — only for the exact app/data/cache figures described above. Without it the app shows the APK size it can already see and nothing else changes.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (optional) — only if you enable the status bar notification.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (optional) — only to restore that notification after a restart, if you enabled it.
  • com.android.launcher.permission.INSTALL_SHORTCUT — only on Android 7 and below, so a shortcut you asked for can be placed on the home screen.

Android shows its own confirmation dialog for every uninstall. The app cannot remove anything silently.

Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties. The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs.

Some screens open external services at your request:

  • Google Play Store (app details, rating)
  • Your email app (feedback)
  • Your system share sheet (sharing the app)
  • System settings (usage access, notifications) when you tap those rows

Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Data retention

The app keeps no server-side data. Locally stored settings remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Security

Because no data is transmitted, there is no server-side store to breach. Local settings use Android's app-private storage.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or any feature that changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date will change.
